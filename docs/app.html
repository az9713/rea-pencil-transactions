What to expect from this release

What you get
A standalone Windows/browser editor for a limited part of the .pen 2.21 format. Staging, rough previews and undo history live in this tool, outside Pencil.
Verified so far
Supported rename, property and move edits, grouped-change checks, undo/redo and repeat edits were tested with fixtures, the browser and packaged app.
Still needs testing or implementation
Reopening edited files in the real Pencil app and comparing its rendered result remain unverified.
Relationship to the original app
Installing it adds no controls to Pencil. The previews are simple outlines. A workflow inside Pencil and reliable exchange of broader documents would require additional host and format integration work.
